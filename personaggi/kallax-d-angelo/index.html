Abilità

  • Armeggiare magico (3 usi): Artefice (Magical Tinkering). Con un'azione tocca un piccolo oggetto non magico e gli dà una proprietà: emette luce (intensa per 1,5 m e fioca per altri 1,5 m); quando viene toccato riproduce un messaggio registrato di al massimo 6 secondi, udibile fino a 3 m; emette di continuo un odore o un suono non verbale, percepibile fino a 3 m; mostra un'immagine statica (un disegno, fino a 25 parole di testo, linee e forme). La proprietà dura per sempre; con un'azione può toccare l'oggetto e farla finire. Può avere al massimo 3 oggetti così (il suo modificatore di Intelligenza).
  • Infondere oggetti (2 usi, riposo lungo): Artefice (Infuse Item). Conosce 4 infusioni: alla fine di un riposo lungo può infonderle in oggetti non magici, che diventano oggetti magici. Al massimo 2 oggetti infusi alla volta.
  • Lo strumento giusto: Artefice (The Right Tool for the Job). Con gli arnesi da inventore in mano crea per magia un set di strumenti da artigiano a scelta. Ci vuole un'ora, che può coincidere con un riposo breve o lungo. Lo strumento sparisce quando ne crea un altro.
  • Celebrare rituali: Può lanciare come rituale, senza spendere slot, gli incantesimi da artefice che ha preparato e che hanno l'etichetta rituale: il lancio richiede 10 minuti in più.
  • Macchina fotografica arcana: Fotografo, sottoclasse homebrew. Ha costruito la sua macchina fotografica: un giorno di lavoro, 20 mo di metallo, fili e ingranaggi e una gemma di Asprite. La macchina gli fa da focus arcano e ha due modalità a scelta (vedi "La classe del Fotografo" qui sotto).
  • Foto magiche (1 uso, riposo breve): Fotografo, dal 5° livello. Con una gemma in più nella macchina può fotografare una creatura o un oggetto e sviluppare subito la foto, che è un oggetto magico consumabile. Con la Pepsina la foto crea un'immagine maggiore di ciò che ritrae; con l'Hola un'effigie d'inchiostro con le statistiche della creatura (niente incantesimi, un solo attacco, 4 PF per livello da artefice); con la Fantina può travestirsi da ciò che ha fotografato. Ogni uso consuma un frammento della gemma (una gemma ne ha 4).
  • Incantesimi del fotografo: Fotografo. Ha sempre preparati cecità/sordità e vedere l'invisibilità, che non contano nel numero degli incantesimi preparati. È anche competente negli arnesi da inventore.
  • Attacco furtivo (1 volta per turno): Ladro. +1d6 ai danni di un attacco con un'arma accurata o a distanza, se ha vantaggio o se un alleato è entro 1,5 m dal bersaglio (e lui non ha svantaggio).
  • Azione scaltra: Ladro. Può disimpegnarsi, scattare o nascondersi con un'azione bonus.
  • Maestria: Ladro. Bonus di competenza doppio per Indagare e Furtività (+9).

BACKSTORY

2am: KALES

In the middle of the night, there is only one little light flickering at the offices of the Herald of Kales. A single lightbulb breaks the darkness of the cold winter nights, and it illuminates a young man sitting in front of his arcane writing machine. The man in question, a handsome young human, probably in his early thirties, with sun-kissed skin tone and gorgeous long black hair meticulously styled to look effortlessly messy, seems focused on his work. Actually, effortless is probably one of the best words to describe this man. His robes, a combination of a teal jacket and technical gear, effortlessly match his eye colours (one blue and one green), and the whole look seems to accentuate, without even trying, the many physical qualities of the man known to be as Kallax d’Angello. Okay, my dear reader, you may now be confused as to why am I writing a chronicle and interview about myself. Well, let’s say that every journal of a certain prestige needs to listen to its readers, and our public from the gossip section has been crying for an article about Kales’ hottest court member. And who am I to not comply with your wishes… (green-eye wink) So here I am, alone at my office, in front of my arcane writing machine, my invention by the way, preparing and documenting for what could be the most relevant and interesting interview of my life. It requires a whole lot of preparation to interview someone, and research about their origin, intimate circles, goals in life, and recent achievements. But in this case, it is completely different than that. I already know who I am, this interview should go somewhere else:

Everyone talks about Kallax d’Angello, the new sensation of the court of Kales, but how did you become the person you are today? Well, let’s say that my life was, of course not a piece of cake. I was born in a “moderately” wealthy family from Kales’s provinces, of course, nothing special, just the main extractors of gems in the region (the Gioello family). As their 3rd son, I was considered completely politically irrelevant and thus, I was ignored both by my parents and siblings. Well, that was until they discovered I was good at using my hands. [Take some notes single ladies and foes from Kales]. Okay, what I mean by that is that they discovered I had spent my time “tinkering”, trying to create artificial constructions powered by gems. Of course, my creations did not work at the time. I am a genius, but meddling with the forces of nature and mankind takes time and more ability than I had at that moment. However, my family saw in that an opportunity for me to become finally of use and expand the family business into the “artificier craze” that was starting to populate Kales City. Aka get rid of me for some years, forever if possible. That is why I was sent to the prestigious Kales Academy to become an artificer.

I checked the records of the Academy and there is no artificer with the Gioello or di Angello surname. Oh, I see you’ve done your homework. Well, that is simply because I never graduated. I arrived at the Academy with the illusion of finally meeting people interested in pushing the boundaries of artificial, mingling with arcane secrets and making the world a better place. Who would have said that teachers would only be interested in building robots for political purposes, and students would only want to suck the teacher’s dicks (sadly only metaphorically) to secure themselves a stable position in the future? Well, I guess my time in the academy was not completely in vain. I refined my craftsmanship up to a level higher than any master there could teach me, discovered my profound and absolute love for nicely tailored suits, and managed to blow up what potentially could have become a breakthrough in a war I didn’t even know existed at the moment. For that, I was expelled from the academy and my records were completely removed from the archives. After all, how can you justify expelling a student for destroying a thing that doesn’t exist, to fight in a war people don’t even know about? The day I was expelled from the academy, my family made it very clear that their 3rd son died with the death of their political pretensions and that I should never be seen again. That day, Federicco Gioello died and Kallax d’Angello was born.

How did you go from that to the court of Kales? Alone, broke and resourceless I only had a new goal: To Survive. I guess this was even more difficult when people from the academy were actively trying to kill you throughout all the undergrounds of Kales. This period taught me several important things, the power of secrets, that my face was worth quite some money, and that what I had done in the Academy was going to chase me for life.

My pretty face kept me alive during the toughest months, but afterwards, my privileged abilities to extract information allowed me to secure a stable position in the underskirts of Kales. When shady people need information they “pray to the Angels”, and sometimes the angels reply back, for a modest price of course. The Angels soon became my family and my job, to protect them at all costs. Protect them from what happened in the underground of the city, and also from what could happen in a war that they didn’t even know existed. To that end, we expanded our domain from the darkness to the high spheres. I founded the Herald of Kales and started participating in the court intrigues. Of course, soon I became beloved and wanted by most of the court members. And, as in my times of youth, they told me secrets that I could redistribute when I considered necessary. Some of these secrets were glimpses of a bigger issue, a war that the master artificers didn’t want me to know about.

What war? A war longer than time, that repeats itself, and always ends in the same way: death for all of us. A war where kings and queens are only mere pawns of creatures as powerful as gods. There are only two alternatives, to become a god or to be useful to them. I left kales to find the power to become a god and returned with the key for being useful to them.

You found something special, right? In my travels, I discovered a thing. Something that is the key to my survival and the survival of my circle. It is exactly what I was dreaming of since my youth. The proof that arcane magic and artificer devices can coexist. Just the mere confirmation of this could be revolutionary, but this artefact is much more than that. It is the key to becoming useful to a god. Who knows, maybe like this they will allow us to survive.

Why becoming so open about everything now? Well, I am taking a huge bet. In the best-case scenario, this interview will never reach the light of day because I’ll return safe and sound to cancel the sending spell I have programmed to distribute to all our readers by midday tomorrow. In the worst case, I am dead and everyone should know what is going on. Look for the signs. Kales’ government is not worried about you. Only about a future that is to come where you will die for sure. Our ruler Kales is one of the players in this war and I have evidence of another one. It is someone known as The Crow and you’ll have more updates soon enough. Try your best to enjoy your last days my readers.

Regards from hell,

Kallax d’Angello

Abilità homebrew

La classe del Fotografo

Sottoclasse homebrew dell’Artefice. Il fotografo usa la magia per catturare la realtà.

  • Competenza negli strumenti (3° livello). Competenza negli arnesi da inventore; se ce l’ha già, in un altro strumento da artigiano a scelta.
  • Incantesimi del fotografo (3° livello). Sempre preparati, non contano nel numero degli incantesimi preparati:
    • 5° livello: cecità/sordità, vedere l’invisibilità;
    • 9° livello: immagine maggiore, trama ipnotica.
  • La macchina fotografica arcana (3° livello). In un giorno di lavoro, con 20 mo di metallo, fili e componenti meccanici e una gemma di Asprite, costruisce la sua macchina fotografica. Gli fa da focus arcano. Quando la costruisce sceglie due modalità:
    • Flash. Colpisce una creatura entro 3 m con un lampo di luce: tiro salvezza su Costituzione o è accecata per 1 turno.
    • Zoom. Il prossimo incantesimo con gittata di 1,5 m o più può raddoppiare la gittata, spendendo una carica della macchina. Se la gittata è a contatto, diventa 9 m.
    • Filtri Instagram. Il prossimo incantesimo che fa danni elementali può cambiare tipo di danno: acido, freddo, fuoco, fulmine, veleno o tuono.
    • Timer. Sceglie un incantesimo che sa lanciare e fa partire un timer, da 10 secondi a 10 minuti: alla fine lancia l’incantesimo senza componenti verbali, somatiche o materiali.
    • Raffica. Il prossimo incantesimo con tempo di lancio di 1 azione lo può lanciare con un’azione bonus.
  • Foto magiche (5° livello). Usa il potere delle altre tre gemme: aggiunge una gemma alla macchina e può fotografare una creatura o un oggetto e sviluppare subito la foto, che è un oggetto magico consumabile.
    • Pepsina: la foto, consumata, crea un’immagine maggiore della creatura o dell’oggetto in un punto a scelta entro 9 m.
    • Hola: crea un’effigie d’inchiostro della creatura o dell’oggetto. Ha le stesse statistiche, ma non lancia incantesimi, fa un solo attacco e ha 4 PF per livello da artefice.
    • Fantina: può travestirsi da ciò che ha fotografato.
    • Ogni uso consuma un frammento della gemma, e non può usare di nuovo questa capacità fino a un riposo breve. Una gemma vale quattro frammenti.
